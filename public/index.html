Manage your dorm life in one place

DormFlow helps students of the residence report maintenance problems and book shared facilities. Administrators handle requests, manage rooms and post announcements.

What you can do

Report maintenance

Submit a repair request for plumbing, heating, electricity and more, then track its status.

Book facilities

Reserve the laundry room, study room or gym in free time slots without overlaps.

Read announcements

Stay up to date with news and important notices from the residence administration.
